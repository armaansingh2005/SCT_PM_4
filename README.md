# 📋 Product Requirements Document (PRD)

A product management project focused on defining product vision, identifying user needs, documenting functional requirements, and establishing a structured roadmap for successful product development.

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Product Requirements Document (PRD)** project demonstrates the process of translating product ideas and business objectives into clear, actionable product requirements.

A PRD serves as a foundation for product development by explaining what needs to be built, why it matters, who the target users are, and how the product should function.

This project focuses on structured product documentation, requirement analysis, feature definition, and stakeholder alignment.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Understand the fundamentals of product management.
- Define product vision, goals, and objectives.
- Identify target users and their requirements.
- Document functional and non-functional requirements.
- Define product features and priorities.
- Establish clear product scope and expectations.
- Improve communication between product and development teams.
- Create a structured foundation for product development.

## ✨ Key Components of a PRD

### 1. Product Overview
Provides an introduction to the product, its purpose, and its overall vision.

### 2. Problem Statement
Identifies the specific problem the product aims to solve.

### 3. Target Audience
Defines the intended users and their needs, expectations, and challenges.

### 4. Product Goals
Establishes measurable objectives that guide product development.

### 5. Functional Requirements
Describes the features, capabilities, and expected behavior of the product.

### 6. Non-Functional Requirements
Defines quality expectations such as performance, usability, reliability, and security.

### 7. User Stories
Describes product functionality from the user's perspective.

### 8. Feature Prioritization
Organizes product features according to their importance and business value.

### 9. Success Metrics
Identifies key performance indicators (KPIs) used to evaluate product success.

### 10. Product Scope
Defines what is included in the product and establishes development boundaries.

## 🔄 Product Management Workflow

1. Understand the business problem.
2. Identify user needs and pain points.
3. Define the product vision and objectives.
4. Research the target audience.
5. Document functional and non-functional requirements.
6. Develop user stories and feature descriptions.
7. Prioritize product features.
8. Define success metrics.
9. Prepare the final Product Requirements Document.
